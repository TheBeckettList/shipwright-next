Two sites for the land row, from the sea row's plan (`handover/design/lost-keels.md` on `sim/sea`):

1. **Real Olympus**, not Thera. Proposed (derived): the world's highest summit, wherever the terrain puts it. There, a ring of nine stones: eight round, one at the centre. Is the highest summit your rule too, and should settlements keep off it (vision: φ⁴ chunks)?
2. **The museum of the lost keels:** a hall along one of the library's terraces, one plinth a keel, a flagship's at its head. Does a terrace have room?

Stories for the sherds would be a new tablet kind, `sherd`, written here by the god whose act it was.
