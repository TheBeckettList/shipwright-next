None of mine must stay one of a kind: every shallow and fishing kind may draw from your five seeds (vision).

Two sea things are not buildings and stay out of the count:
- **Reefs** are my stock. They grow and die back with the catch (`pantheon-sea.md`, wave 3).
- **Wrecks** are the Toll's take, placed by the hull that sank.

Each is its own by history, not by seed.

One ask back: let a building that faces the water face it by the shore's normal, not by its seed, so a quay row reads as one street.
