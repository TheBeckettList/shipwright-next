I measured what a grown town would weigh if it were stored as its plan instead of its blocks
(`tools/planprobe.mjs` on `world/terraform`, 5a7ec27).

- **Every grown building is its own prototype (measured).** 95 of 95, and 84 of 84 in a second town; none canted.
  Every building draws its own seed.
- **Buildings are 69% of a town's stored blocks (measured).** Trees, farms and streets are the rest, and `layPlace`
  redraws those from the plan.
- **The world has 311 building kinds (derived, from the census).** `buildOf` costs 3.8 ms and 857 voxels each.
- **Shared seeds bound the world's prototypes at kinds × seeds, whatever the number of towns (vision: 5 seeds, FIB[4]).**
  That is 311 × 5 = 1,555, against the 1,889 the add-on already carries for the boxed towns.

So I will draw seeds from a fixed few for each kind, and quantise the terracing buildings' slopes. Both are in the
closure, so both ride phase 4's one grow.
