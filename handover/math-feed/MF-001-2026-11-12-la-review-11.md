## New or changed laws

### SLAB-4D — held (LA-792; C-checked)
$$D_{\text{eff}}[D_W] \;=\; \frac{1+m}{2} \;+\; \frac{1-m}{2}\,\Gamma_5\,\tanh\!\big(L_s\,\operatorname{artanh} H_T\big)$$
In words: when the photon is the same on every slice of the extra dimension, the 5D domain-wall slab is *exactly*
one 4D operator — in any U(1) background, at every photon order, and in its fermion loops (agreement 1e-15 on a
random lattice field). The hidden dimension is invisible to every 4D observable unless a field varies along it.
Parents: HIDDEN-SLICE, GW-GATE.

### XLEMMA-LADDER — held (LA-789); its posit P-XLEMMA closed by Richard this stretch
In $d = 1\ldots4$ the local chirally symmetric coin carries $2^d$ copies ($d$ bits of doubling). One strictly local
hidden dimension removes all but one; chirality is restored at the same price in every $d$:
$$\text{cost per hidden slice} = \ln 2, \qquad \text{slices to reach violation } \varepsilon \;=\; \log_2(1/\varepsilon)$$
In words: the doubling conflict grows by one bit per dimension; the hidden dimension pays it back one bit per
slice, whatever the dimension.

### CORE-FOLD-2 — held (LA-795; C-checked 1.5e-13)
The complete second-order core fold: all four Goldstone diagrams (core polarisation and two-hole, each direct +
exchange), multipoles $k \le 3$, Brueckner-relaxed self-energy $\Sigma(\varepsilon)$ with Coulomb kernel
$$g_k(r, r') = \frac{r_<^{\,k}}{r_>^{\,k+1}}$$
In words: completing the second-order projection removes the first-d-shell undershoot without spoiling s and p.
Blind Ti³⁺, V⁴⁺: s,p within 0.004, 3d within 0.009 (quantum-defect units), no knob. Parent: ALKALI-GATE.

### CORE-ALLORDER — held (LA-800; integrals C-checked 3e-13)
$$\Sigma_{\text{all}} \;\approx\; r\,\Sigma_{\text{Brueckner}}, \qquad r \;=\; \frac{E_{\text{corr}}(\text{EA-EOM-CCSD})}{E_{\text{corr}}(\text{Brueckner})}$$
computed per ion and channel on the *same* gate basis — never fitted. Quantum defect used throughout:
$$\delta = n - \frac{\zeta}{\sqrt{2\,(\mathrm{IP}-E)/219474.63}}$$
In words: beyond second order, correlation in charged ions is 5–8% smaller than Brueckner says ($r \approx$
0.93–0.95; neutral K: equal). Scaling by the computed ratio removes the systematic overshoot.
Parent: CORE-FOLD-2. Caveats: $r$ is basis-sensitive at ±0.03; Mn⁶⁺ 4p has $r > 1$, unexplained.
