# Onboarding: the world architect (towns, cities, landscape)

_For the developer who builds Waterworld's settlements, buildings and terrain from S003 on, and for their Claude Code.
Written 2026-11-13 by Richard's assistant. Read this, then `COLLABORATING.md`, then `handover/AI_BRIEF.md`._

## Your role

You own **the built world**: where peoples settle, how their towns and cities grow, what their buildings look like,
how the ground meets them, and Thera, the capital island at the heart of the world. Richard and his assistant own
**the ships** (`ship/`, `core/boat.js`). The two of you work at the same time, on separate branches, and meet in the
stage branch through pull requests (`COLLABORATING.md`).

## What this project is, in one minute

- **Waterworld** is a Minecraft Bedrock add-on. Its world isn't generated by Minecraft: it's laid chunk by chunk from
  this repo's own math, in a dimension of its own. **Shipwright** (historical boatbuilding and sailing) is folded in as
  its ship module.
- **The sim and the game are two ways into one world** (D-003). Everything runs in Node first (`core/`, `world/`), is
  checked by "lenses" (`node tools/lab.mjs`), and is then laid in the game by thin glue (`game/`, `mod/`).
- **The map is static and painted:** `world/map.png`, 2,048 × 1,024, one pixel per chunk. It's about 88% sea, with
  243 islands. Nothing is seeded.
- **Inherency is the law of the codebase.** Every number is a `given` (Minecraft's, the Earth's, geometry's), a
  `vision` (our choice, with its reason) or `derived`. Constants live in `core/given.js`. Anywhere else a number
  carries a `// given:`, `// vision:` or `// derived:` tag on its line. The `inherency` lens counts untagged numbers,
  and its ratchet is 0. The golden law (φ, Fibonacci, `core/golden.js`) is the preferred source of proportions:
  bounds like `FIB[4]` = 5 blocks show up everywhere.
- **Towns are grown, not drawn.** `world/cities.js` picks the sites, `world/growth.js` grows them, and
  `world/district.js` lays them. The grown towns are baked by `tools/bakegrown.mjs` into `lab/grown/` (one file per
  town), keyed by a hash of every source they import. Touch their closure (`world/cities.js`, `world/district.js`,
  `game/ground.js` and everything they import) and the towns go stale and need a regrow (about 55 min). Batch the
  changes, then regrow once.

## Your first job: S004, terraforming and Thera

Richard's brief, verbatim, is in `handover/design/s004-specs/BRIEF.md`. Two design specs are ready and measured
against the laid world. Build from them, in this order:

1. **`design/s004-specs/terraform.md`, the overlay law.** Today Thera's houses and the boxed towns get buried (221
   shallow houses entombed on Thera, 318 of 697 carved houses blocked at floor level), stand on marble cubes (the
   Library on a 33-high calcite footing) or on stilts over the deep. The fix is a pure field T(x, z): the natural
   ground clamped by each building's pad. The pad's level is the median of the ground under it, so cut balances fill,
   with cut and fill each bounded by `FIB[4]`. Banks run round each pad, and there are aprons at the doors. The spec
   then gives each people's land use around their houses (yards, terraces, gardens by tradition, living and climate),
   the lens checks to write, and the build order. Grown towns are already sound; they gain yards.
2. **`design/s004-specs/thera.md`, Thera per D-006.** The cone island grows to radius × 1.5 and 25% higher, open at
   the top (a dormant volcano). A ring waterfront round it carries the cothon (half cut into the rock) and the League's
   Thera HQ and Mint (cothon-sized). The spec continues:
   - The Fallen Star: a meteor under the cone, offset away from the cothon, with its ore dispersion zone under the
     caldera (`world/impact.js`, new).
   - The tunnels as a math generator, not hand-placed: lava tubes plus pre-Minoan, Çatalhöyük-style ruins
     (`world/undercone.js`, new).
   - The Therans' automining refinery, and the ore carried to the Mint, where emeralds become stamped metal.
   - Why the Therans guard it all: the mythos, built from inspiration, not aliens.
   - Fewer marble cubes, more paths and less diorite on the inner cone.
3. **One regrow** of the grown towns, after both: the peoples layer was made less griddy in S003 (territories grown
   over the land), and those towns are stale until the regrow.

Log what you decide in `handover/DECISIONS.md` and what goes wrong in `handover/ISSUES.md` as you go, not at the end.
Keep a stage file: copy `handover/templates/STAGE_TEMPLATE.md` to `handover/stages/S004-<date>-W.md` (your stream's
code is W; Richard's is R) and keep it current. Don't use `handover/bin/new-stage.sh` for now: it makes a `stage/...`
branch, and those take pull requests only, so you couldn't push to it. Work on `world/<topic>` branches instead.

## Where you touch the ships

- **`world/boatsizes.js`**: the size laws every people's boats follow, and what they ask of the shore (slip and
  harbour depths). Docks, harbours and boathouses should read their sizes from it, never hard-code a boat.
- **`world/docks.js`, `world/harbour.js`**: where ships berth. Richard's stream is redesigning the Theran fleet (the
  Exokoitos glider, a catamaran and a trimaran flagship) and the League's galleys, which are all great ships sized per
  trade route. Ask before changing berth geometry; say so in your PR.
- **Thera's cothon and the League's Mint**: the Therans' ships dock there, and the League's economy runs through it.
- `core/given.js` is shared: add constants, don't change the ships' ones.

## Rules that bite

- `core/` stays pure: no Minecraft imports, no untagged numbers.
- **Never hand-edit baked data** (`BP/scripts/data/`, `game/calibration.js`, `lab/grown/`): regenerate it.
- **Vanilla is the bound:** every block, item and mob is checked against Bedrock's own lists (`lab/bedrock_*.json`,
  the `vanilla` lens).
- **Never run `ww new`**, or anything else that deletes worlds or files, without asking first (ISSUES I-005).
- Never run `ship/tools/gen_entities.py`. Back up `ship/RP/models/entity/shield_peg.geo.json` around
  `ship/tools/gen_assets.py`. (You shouldn't need either; they're the ships' tools.)
- Builds stay out of git. The version (`tools/buildmod.mjs`, `mod/scripts/main.js`) is bumped once per stage hand-off.
- Say what you verified and what you didn't. "Not tested in game" is a fine thing to write; a guess written as a
  fact is not. The last build (0.9.14) hasn't been loaded in the game yet.
